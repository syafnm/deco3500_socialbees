How a session runs

  1. Students join by scanning your QR code and entering their name and student ID.
  2. They answer two questions. Interest gives them a colour, working style gives them an icon.
  3. Matching. They walk around and pair up with anyone. A pair forms when both enter each other's PIN.
  4. Conversations. Each pair gets three questions based on their cards, and you run the timer.
  5. Next round. They keep their colour and pick a working style again.
  6. Teams. After each round students say who they'd team up with. You build teams from the mutual matches and confirm them.
