## **4\. Conflict Resolution Plan**

**What counts as a conflict for this team:**

- Weak/incomplete output: work submitted that doesn't meet an agreed standard or doesn't fulfil the task as scoped
- Late delivery: missing an agreed internal deadline without prior warning
- Unequal contribution: consistent imbalance in effort relative to assigned duties
- Interpersonal friction: disagreements that affect how the team works together
- Failing to communicate when unable to complete a task

**How issues get raised:**

1. The person who notices the issue raises it directly and privately with the individual first, assuming good faith rather than malice.
2. If unresolved or recurring, the issue is raised at the next team meeting as an agenda item.
3. The team discusses the issue and agrees on a resolution.
4. What was discussed and agreed is documented in the meeting minutes.
5. If the issue cannot be resolved internally, the team seeks guidance from the teaching staff.

What happens if it can't be resolved internally: If the issue remains unresolved after discussion within the team, the Team Lead facilitates a structured conversation with all members present. If it is still unresolved, the team jointly escalates the issue to the teaching staff, providing a summary of the issue and the steps already taken to resolve it. Escalation is treated as a last resort.
