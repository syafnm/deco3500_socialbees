# Part 4 — Discussion About Team Formation

**Participant:**
So is the goal to form teams for assignments?

**Facilitator:**
Not exactly.

The idea isn't necessarily to directly form the team. The goal is to make students more comfortable interacting with people before forming a team.

Sometimes approaching new people can feel awkward or uncomfortable.

The activity gives students something to discuss. By discussing the clues and solving the activity together, they can start building a connection.

**Participant:**
So it's like getting to know each other before forming the group?

**Facilitator:**
Exactly.
